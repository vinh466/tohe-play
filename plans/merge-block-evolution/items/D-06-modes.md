# D-06 — The modes and the title/mode select

Status: open (blocked by D-01). Kind: decision.

## Question

Which modes exist in the first slice, and how does the title present them without becoming a wall
of cards?

## The modes

| Mode | What it is | Ranked? | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cổ điển** | Today's endless run; the ranked, verified game; the challenge | Yes | 2.1 (unchanged) |
| **Ca làm** (Orders) | 3–5 orders, 1-of-3 parts, the jam/clock-out, the scrap | No | 2.1 |
| **Ca hôm nay** (Daily) | One seeded run per local day, streak, share | No (local) | 2.2 |
| **Thư giãn** (Zen) | The rack without pressure/jam; free play | No | 2.3 |
| **Đấu bóng** (Ghost/Versus) | The challenge/async already gives a versus; real-time rooms are a later plan | No | after 3.0 |

## Options

1. **Classic + Orders in 2.1, Daily + Zen later** (recommended): the title has a primary
   (Continue/Play) + the mode cards; the Daily card appears in 2.2; the Zen in 2.3.
2. **All four from 2.1**: the daily/zen are small but the title is busier; the first slice is
   bigger.
3. **Classic only, the others behind a "Ca làm" hub**: one door, then the mode select. Cleaner
   title, one extra tap.
4. **A full mode-select screen** after the title: the title is the brand, the mode select is a
   separate screen. More taps; a stronger brand moment.

## Recommendation

1: the title is **one primary + a compact mode row** (Classic, Orders; the Daily card; the Workshop;
the leaderboard; the settings; the how-to; the "Về Tò He"). The primary is "Chơi tiếp" when a run is
in progress, otherwise "Chơi" (the last mode or the classic). Each mode card shows its best/record
and an "xếp hạng"/"không xếp hạng" mark. The Daily card is visually a date stamp; the Zen a small
leaf. The title fits 320 with the cards stacked; the brand mark/wordmark at the top. A newcomer's
first "Chơi" runs the onboarding; the "Cổ điển" is the same game as today.

## Cost and risks

The menu-stage layout (the kit's shell), the mode state/route, the labels, the first-run, the 320/
light/dark screenshots. The risk is the title becoming a dashboard; the primary + four compact
cards is the limit; the "coming soon" cards are avoided (the Daily/Zen appear when they ship).

## What would change my mind

- The owner wants the brand to be the first screen: 4 (a dedicated mode-select screen) with the
  title as the brand/Play.
- The family only plays the classic: the Orders/Daily move behind a "Ca làm" hub (3).
