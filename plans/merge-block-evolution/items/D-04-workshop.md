# D-04 — The Workshop and its light meta

Status: open (blocked by D-01, D-12). Kind: decision.

## Question

What is the long-term progression: what does scrap buy, what persists per profile, how does it stay
light (no IAP/grind/timers) and not make the ranked classic easier?

## Options

| Option | What persists | The pull | The risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Light (recommended)** | Scrap; blueprints (the part pool); machine levels (Tập sự → Kỹ sư, XP); cosmetics (the machine skin, the chute, the block palette, the stamps); stat sidegrades **Orders-only** | A reason to return, an identity, no grind, the classic untouched | The meta may feel thin for an adult; the cosmetics need enough variety |
| B. Full stat meta | Permanent stats (pressure max, chute, etc.) for every mode | Strong progression | Breaks the classic/ranked fairness; a grind |
| C. Cosmetics only | The skins/stamps/palettes; no parts/levels | Safe, premium | Little progression; the Orders/parts carry it |
| D. No meta | The Orders/Daily only | Smallest | No long-term reason; the Workshop disappears |

## Recommendation

**A.** The Workshop's first slice: the scrap counter; the **three blueprint slots** (the parts the
Orders can offer); the **machine level** (XP from runs/orders; a cosmetic plaque + a small
unlock); the **cosmetics tab** (the machine skin, the chute style, the palette — the palette is the
existing setting). The stat sidegrades are the **Orders' parts** (D-03) and never apply to the
classic. The scrap is earned by playing (shipped blocks, orders, the daily), not bought; no energy,
no timers, no ads. The Workshop is reachable from the title and the ticket; the first visit is
after the first order.

The machine levels: 5–6 ranks (Tập sự, Thợ phụ, Thợ chính, Trưởng ca, Kỹ sư, Bậc thầy) with a
short line each; the XP from a run's orders/ships; no stat power, only the unlocks/cosmetics; the
child sees the rank, not a battle pass.

## Cost and risks

The scrap/XP/level state, the blueprint pool, the cosmetics, the screen, the save, the tests. The
risk is the meta becoming a chore or the cosmetics becoming a shop; the copy and the "no money"
design are explicit. The classic's best/ranked stay separate; the Workshop's stats are labelled
Orders-only.

## What would change my mind

- The family wants a deeper meta: B, but only in a **separate workshop run** (never the classic).
- The cosmetics are not enough: the stamps/achievements (a collection) instead of stat power.
