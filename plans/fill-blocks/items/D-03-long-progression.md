# D-03 — Long progression model

**Status:** decided — owner approved the recommended option on 2026-11-08 ("ok tốt nha, làm đi") · **Kind:** decision · **Blocked by:** D-01

## Question

What gives Lấp Ô a long progression without ads, energy, FOMO or a punishing streak?

## Options

### A — Hybrid: endless + journey + daily + mastery + achievements (recommended)

| Layer | Long-term driver | Pressure |
|---|---|---|
| **Chơi tự do** (endless) | Personal best; mastery rank; stats | None |
| **Hành trình** (journey) | 18–36 levels, 3 stars, chapter unlocks | None; replay any level |
| **Thử thách hôm nay** (daily) | One seed/day, calendar stamp, share | None; missing a day costs nothing |
| **Thành tích** | 15 achievements; totals; board skins | None |

The journey is the spine; endless is the pure game; daily is the gentle return; ranks/achievements are
recognition. All are optional except the core.

**Cost:** content design and a shell screen (E-01); the most work of the options, but it is the only one that
matches "tiến trình dài" without pressure.

### B — Endless + mastery ranks + achievements only

- Simpler: no level content, no journey screen. Ranks and achievements give long-term goals.
- Risk: after the first few ranks, the game is "just one more score"; the owner asked for a long progression,
  and the genre's strongest pattern is a curated journey.

### C — Journey only

- Curated levels, no endless. Easier to scope, but loses the "open and play" loop and the personal-best chase;
  a player who finishes the journey has nowhere to go.

### D — Seasonal/battle pass/energy (reject)

- Proven in the market, but it is pressure/FOMO and contradicts `docs/vision.md`. Reject.

## Recommendation

**A**, phased: V1 endless + save/scores; V2 journey + ranks + stats; V3 daily + achievements + skins; V4 platform
daily/ranked. This lets the core ship and be tested before the meta layer is built.

## What "long" means

- **Session**: 1–5 minutes; a journey level 1–3.
- **Week**: the journey's next chapter + a daily stamp + a rank step.
- **Month**: 36 levels, 10 ranks, 15 achievements, 6 skins.
- **No infinite grind**: after the journey, the endless mode and mastery ranks remain; no daily obligation.

## Evidence

- Adventure/level maps and daily challenges are the genre's strongest retention patterns:
  [R-01](../research/R-01-block-puzzle-market.md).
- Mastery, autonomy and relatedness; avoid loss aversion/FOMO: [R-02](../research/R-02-ux-and-retention.md).
- The shell has no journey screen today: [R-03](../research/R-03-platform-and-reuse.md).

## Discussion

**agent (2026-11-08):** recommend A. The market shows the core loop is the product; the journey is the best
long-term layer that does not require pressure. The daily should be a calendar, not a streak.
