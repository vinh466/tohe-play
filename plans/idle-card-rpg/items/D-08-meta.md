# D-08 — Meta: no home, collection and codex (decided)

**Status:** decided — owner chose **Codex + bonds + rank** on 2026-11-09 · **Kind:** decision · **Blocked by:** D-01, D-06

## Decisions (owner's words)

> "k có nhà, trên main zone là khu vực đánh nhau luôn kiểu legends of mushroom luôn đánh qua màn ấy, còn phong cách thì là dạng board các card đánh nhau"

> "Codex + bonds + rank (Recommended)"

- **No home, no camp, no caravan screen.**
- The **main zone is the battle board**; the company is always fighting through stages.
- All meta lives in **panels over the board**.
- **Codex**: 16 hero entries, 16 enemy entries, 4 region entries; unlocked by meeting/defeating; small milestone bonuses.
- **Bonds**: one bond pair per hero (8 pairs for 16 heroes); deployed together, a small bonus; a short story line in the codex.
- **Company rank**: account progression from stage clears; raises the shared hero level cap and offline rate.
- **Records**: best stage, tower floor, boss damage; local only (no public board in v1).

## What replaces the home

The home used to carry four jobs; each needs a new place:

| Job of a home | New place |
|---|---|
| Idle rewards / "come back" | The **offline return**: the company keeps fighting while away; a summary panel on return (12 h cap). |
| Long-term progression sink | **Company rank** + **gear/star** + **codex**; no buildings. |
| Collection showcase | **Codex**: hero/enemy/region entries, bonds, records; a panel. |
| Daily tasks | A **daily sheet** over the board, with a badge when something is ready. |

## Options for the meta layer

### A — Codex + bonds + company rank (recommended for v1)

- **Codex**: one entry per hero, enemy and region; unlocked by meeting/defeating them; small permanent bonuses for milestones.
- **Bonds**: each hero has one bond partner; deployed together, a small stat/skill bonus; a short story line in the codex.
- **Company rank**: account progression from stage clears; raises the shared hero level cap and idle rate.
- **Records**: best stage, tower floor, boss damage, local only (no public board in v1).
- **No base, no buildings, no decoration.**

**Cost:** a codex screen/panel, bond data for 12 heroes, rank curve; no new economy.

### B — Pure collection, no bonds

- Codex only; less writing/data; heroes are stat cards.
- Cheaper, but the collection has less emotional hook; the owner wants "nội dung" and a reason to care.

### C — A light caravan/base layer (rejected by the owner)

- 3 upgrades/buildings; would give a home and a resource sink.
- The owner explicitly said no home; do not reintroduce it through the back door.

## Recommendation

**A**, with a small scope: 16 hero entries, 16 enemy entries, 4 region entries, 8 bond pairs, company rank 1–20, local records. The codex is a **panel**, not a screen with its own navigation; it opens over the board.

## What it changes

- **D-09**: stages/regions are the content spine; the codex indexes them.
- **D-12**: the main screen is the board; codex/daily/records are sheets.
- **D-13**: the codex is a place for card art and pixel portraits; it reuses the battle art.
- **D-16**: no home/camp art budget; the codex and bonds get that budget.

## Discussion

**owner (2026-11-09):** no home; main zone is the fight; board of cards; chose codex + bonds + rank.

**agent (2026-11-09):** recorded. Removing the home simplifies the UX and the art budget; the risk is that the game has no "quiet place" and no long-term sink outside gear/stars. The codex + bonds + company rank are the smallest answer; if playtests show the game needs a home, that is a new decision, not a silent base.
