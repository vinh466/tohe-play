# D-10 — Daily rhythm: a light daily

**Status:** decided — owner chose **Daily nhẹ** on 2026-11-09 · **Kind:** decision · **Blocked by:** D-07, D-09

## Decision (owner's words)

> "Daily nhẹ (Recommended)"

- **3 daily tasks**, each tied to the core loop: clear 3 stages, upgrade gear once, summon once. They are suggestions, not chores; the reward is claimed when 3 are done.
- **1 expedition**: a rotating resource dungeon; one run per day, no energy.
- **1 boss**: defeat any boss (region boss, tower boss, or the world boss when it is open). The world boss itself is weekly (D-09); the daily task counts it when open.
- **No energy, no streak punishment.** A 7-day journey gives rewards but does not reset to zero if a day is missed; it just takes longer.
- **Reset at local midnight.** The daily sheet is a panel over the board; a badge on the dock when something is ready.
- **Time budget**: 5–10 minutes, on top of the auto-advance the player already does.

## Why light

- The game is already an idle auto-battler; a heavy daily would turn it into a second job.
- Tò He's promise is calm; a missed day must not feel like a loss.
- The daily is a **reason to open the app**, not the main progression. The offline return + auto-advance already carry the loop.
- The 7-day journey gives a longer goal without a punitive streak.

## What it changes

- **D-07**: daily is a source of gold/essence/shards; the free pull is part of the tasks.
- **D-09**: the expedition and the world boss are the daily/weekly anchors.
- **D-12**: the daily sheet + dock badge; no home tab.
- **D-16**: the 7-day journey is a v1 retention feature, not a live-ops calendar.

## Open

- Exact 3 tasks and their rewards; the 7-day journey's rewards.
- Whether the daily reset uses the device's local date (yes) and how it behaves on a clock change (clamp).
- Whether the expedition rotates by weekday or by a 3-day cycle.
- Whether the world boss is weekly on a fixed day or a 7-day window.
- No push notifications in v1 (calm tone).
