# D-14 — World, name and tone: Vân Du Ký

**Status:** decided — owner chose **Vân Du Ký** on 2026-11-09; factions, regions and lore are open · **Kind:** decision · **Blocked by:** D-01

## Decision (owner's words)

> "Vân Du Ký"

- **Name**: *Vân Du Ký* — "records of wandering the clouds". A travelogue of a company on the road; poetic, fantasy, Vietnamese.
- **World**: an original fantasy world crossed by a long road; the company travels through regions, each with its own palette, enemies and guardian. No real-world IP, no Vietnamese-folklore retelling.
- **Tone**: hopeful, a little mysterious, warm. Not grimdark, not a power fantasy; the player is the company's leader/chronicler, not a chosen one.
- **Factions** (working): the Company (the player), Sky Nomads, Ancient Constructs, Wild Spirits. The four elements — Ember, Tide, Stone, Gale — are the world's weather/elemental forces, not political factions.
- **Regions**: 3 for v1; each region is a leg of the road with a guardian boss.
- **Title screen**: "Vân Du Ký"; the top strip of the board can show the current region instead of the game name.

## Why this name

- It carries the **journey** and the **cloud/sky** imagery without locking the game to a specific mythology.
- It is short, pronounceable in Vietnamese, and distinct from the generic "Đoàn Thám Hiểm".
- It leaves room for the light, warm Tò He tone and for a future light theme.
- It fits the owner's taste for võ hiệp/tiên hiệp without being a wuxia game.

## Open

- Faction names and the central mystery (why the road exists, what the company seeks).
- Region names and their palettes; the three guardians.
- Hero origins: are they villagers, nomads, constructs, spirits, or a mix?
- The English standard name: keep *Vân Du Ký* as a proper name, or add an English name.
- Logo/mark: a road, a cloud, a card, or a caravan wheel.

## What it changes

- **D-09**: regions/guardians get names and palettes from this world.
- **D-13**: the dark-first palette gets a "dusk road" identity; region accents.
- **D-16**: the title, icon, public page and store copy.
- **D-08**: codex entries are framed as a travelogue (heroes, enemies, regions).
