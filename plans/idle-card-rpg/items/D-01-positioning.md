# D-01 — Positioning, player and promise

**Status:** decided — owner chose **Đoàn Thám Hiểm** on 2026-11-09 · **Kind:** decision · **Blocked by:** —

## Decision (owner's words)

> "Đoàn Thám Hiểm"

The game is a **roaming company**, not a village. There is **no base, no camp, no buildings**. The player leads a company of guardians along a road through an original fantasy world; the company's caravan is the home screen and the diegetic reason for idle supplies. The focus is the **journey, the team and the road**, not building a home.

### The promise (working)

> *"Một đội, một con đường, mỗi ngày đi xa hơn. Nuôi dưỡng những hộ vệ, ghép đội hình, và đưa đoàn đi qua những vùng đất chưa ai đặt chân tới — không quảng cáo, không nạp, không ép giờ."*

- **Session**: 5–10 phút, 2–3 lần/ngày; 20–30 phút khi muốn đẩy chương; idle 12 h; không energy.
- **Home**: the caravan — team, road map, supplies, daily. No buildings; cosmetic changes as the company's rank grows (banners, lanterns) are allowed, but not a base-building system.
- **Progression**: camp/company level (shared), hero star, gear; collection/codex and bonds.
- **Content**: chapters are regions along the road; stages are encounters; bosses are road guardians.
- **Tone**: hopeful, a little mysterious, warm; original fantasy, not Vietnamese folklore-specific.
- **Audience**: 16+ first (the shelf's register), family-friendly content; a child can watch/play with a parent, but the game is not designed for a 3-year-old.

## Why this and not "Làng Mây"

- The owner wants the game to be about **the expedition and the systems**, not about managing a village.
- No base removes a whole meta layer and a screen; the scope goes into battle, progression and content instead.
- The caravan still gives a home and an idle-fiction ("the company travels while you are away") without becoming a second game.
- It keeps the door open for a **cosmetic caravan** later (banners, lanterns, a visible road) without adding buildings.

## What it changes

- **D-08**: no base/camp. Meta = collection, codex, bonds, company rank, local records. The "farm" feeling comes from gear/stage farming + the caravan's idle supplies.
- **D-09**: chapters are regions on a road, not islands; the tower is a landmark on the road; the world boss is a shadow that follows the company.
- **D-12**: the home tab is **Đoàn** (caravan), not **Làng/Camp**; tabs become Đoàn · Hành Trình · Anh Hùng · Triệu Hồi + More.
- **D-14**: world = a long road through a shattered original fantasy world; name still open.
- **D-16**: v1 scope loses the camp screens/buildings; that budget goes to heroes, stages and battle polish.
- **D-07**: idle supplies come from the caravan, not buildings; one idle source, not two.

## Options considered (history)

| **Làng Mây** — floating village + camp buildings | Owner chose the expedition without a base. |
| **Đoàn Thám Hiểm** — roaming company, no base | **Chosen.** |

- **D-14**: the name and the world's central mystery.
