# R-09 — UI/UX at 360 × 640: the board, sheets and one hand

Date: 2026-11-09 · Status: first pass, from the first playable

Question: does the 6v6 board and its sheets work on a small phone, and what needs to change before the UI is locked?

## What was tested

- The built game at 390 × 844 in a browser, and the conformance screenshots at 360 × 640.
- The board: 6 enemy cards (back/mid/front) + 6 hero cards (front/mid/back), 2 columns, DOM controls and a 5-item dock.
- The formation sheet, hero sheet (stats/skills/gear), summon sheet and offline card.

## Findings

| Area | Finding | Change |
|---|---|---|
| **Card density** | 12 cards fit; a card is ~167 × 56 CSS px at 390, ~10 px names, HP bar and charge `✦ 0/3`. Readable at 390; at 360 it is tight but workable. | Keep; test at 360 with a real phone. Do not add more card text. |
| **Board hierarchy** | The clash line is clear; enemy back/mid/front read top-to-bottom; hero front/mid/back read toward the clash line. | Add a turn rail (next 6 actors) and an "active card" gold border; already highlighted for the pending hero. |
| **Top strip** | The scene's region label and the DOM stage label are two lines; the region label is behind the Menu button. | One strip: `Menu` + `Vùng 1 · 3/12`; remove the scene's top text. |
| **One hand** | Controls (auto-cast, speed, stop, upgrade) and the dock are in the bottom 15%; touch targets ≥44 px. | Keep; the upgrade action opens the team sheet. |
| **Formation sheet** | 3 × 2 slots + 6 roster rows fit in a scrolling sheet; the selected slot has a gold border; power is visible. | Change "Đang chọn" to "Trong đội"; use role/element words, not ids. |
| **Hero sheet** | Stats, skills and gear fit; star-up and gear + are in the sheet; errors show in place. | Add a back-to-team link, and an "equip" flow later. |
| **Summon** | Pity and shards are visible; ×1 enabled with 100 shards, ×10 must be disabled below 900. | Disable each button by its own cost; add a "earn shards in stages" hint. |
| **Offline card** | One card, one claim; no detour. | Show it only when hours > 0; it is. |
| **Contrast** | The kit's menu button failed WCAG AA until the dark tokens were imported and `data-theme="dark"` set. | Keep the tokens import; conformance passes. |
| **Locale** | The standalone session is English; the hub is Vietnamese first. Role/element ids leaked into the team sheet. | Use `t.roles` / `t.elements` everywhere; the hub locale drives words. |

## Rules for the next screens

1. **The board is the app.** A sheet may cover it, but never replaces it; the dock is the only navigation.
2. **One primary action per sheet.** Lưu đội hình, Nâng ★, Nhận, Triệu hồi — one accent button.
3. **Words before numbers.** A card shows its name and element; the numbers live in the hero sheet.
4. **No hidden state.** A disabled button says why; a wall says what to change.
5. **One hand.** Anything used often sits in the bottom 30%; the Menu and the stage strip are the only top controls.
6. **Dark only, but contrast is still AA.** Import the tokens, set `data-theme="dark"`, and check the conformance contrast pass.
