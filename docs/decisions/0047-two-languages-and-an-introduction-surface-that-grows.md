## Context

- **Languages today.** The app speaks Vietnamese and English, chosen from the browser's language (English only for
  `en*`), with no switch. The public pages, the terms, the privacy notice and the link previews speak Vietnamese only. The
  console's words are Vietnamese, written in its code. Games receive `init.locale`, a free string, and the four games
  carry Vietnamese and English strings; their manifests carry one `name`, in Vietnamese.
- **The introduction surface today** (the public pages, ADR 0042): static Astro pages; JSON-LD per kind of page;
  `llms.txt`; `robots.txt` with Cloudflare's content signals (`search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=no`); a build check
  that fails on a missing title, description, canonical link, preview tags, `<h1>` or structured data. One preview
  picture serves every page, and each game's how-to is written by hand in one file of `apps/web`.
- **Google** asks for one URL per language, a subfolder such as `/en/` being the simplest on one host, `hreflang` in both
  directions declared in one place, a visible switch, and no redirect by language or IP. A `.vn` domain signals Vietnam.
  AI Overviews cite indexed pages and need no special file; the VideoGame type earns no rich result, and FAQ rich results
  were reported ended in 2026; pages made in bulk to rank are spam ("scaled content abuse").
- **AI crawlers** that fetch for answers are separate from those that fetch for training (OpenAI's `OAI-SearchBot` and
  `GPTBot`, Anthropic's `Claude-SearchBot` and `ClaudeBot`; Perplexity's `PerplexityBot` is for answers). Google's
  `Google-Extended` and Apple's `Applebot-Extended` are tokens rather than crawlers: they say whether what Googlebot and
  Applebot fetch may train models, and leave search alone.
- **Vietnamese consumer law** (Law on Protection of Consumers' Rights 2023, article 23) asks for standard terms in
  Vietnamese, other languages allowed, the version more favourable to the consumer applying when they differ (as read
  from summaries of the law, not yet from the official text).
- The owner will next focus on adding games: a new game should bring its public page with it.

## Decision

### Languages

- **Vietnamese is the face, English the support.** Proper names and established terms stay in English inside the
  Vietnamese interface when that is the exact word (game names, "combo", "ranked"…).
- **Public pages**: Vietnamese at `/…` (the URLs already indexed do not change), English at `/en/…` with the same English
  route names; `x-default` → `/en/` — a reader of neither language reads English more easily. `hreflang` is declared in
  `sitemap.xml` only, generated at build time; every page carries its `<html lang>`, `og:locale` and its alternate, a
  canonical link to itself and JSON-LD in its language. The sitemap is generated by `@tohe/routes`, as today, and lists
  each page with its alternates.
- **Switching**: a VI | EN switch in the header leads to the same page in the other language. A browser whose language is
  not Vietnamese gets a suggestion on a Vietnamese page ("View in English →"), never a redirect; a choice is remembered.
- **The app**: the language is a setting (Me → Language), not a path. Without a choice it follows the public page the
  player came from (*Play now* carries it), then the browser (`vi` → Vietnamese, anything else → English), then
  Vietnamese *(agent: the maker of a link or the host of a table does not decide a reader's language)*. A choice on the
  public pages' switch is kept for the app too. Share links and table codes carry no language, so a link sent to a
  family group opens in each reader's language.
- **Link previews** (`/s/…`, `/r/…`) speak the language of the link's maker — a preview is read before anyone opens the
  app, beside the maker's own message: the hub sends its language when it makes a link or opens a table, and the API
  keeps it with the link or the room.
- **Game names**: a manifest's `name` is the game's standard name — English, or a proper name such as *Tiến Lên* —
  required and shown by default; a name per language is optional and shown when present. That changes the Game Contract,
  so it lands with its own ADR, docs and conformance in the pull request that changes the contract. **Descriptions**
  (`description`, one string today) are decided in the same ADR *(open: the owner chooses)*.
- **Games** keep `init.locale`; a game may add a third language on its own; the hub and the public pages speak two.
- **Terms and privacy notice**: the Vietnamese text is the original, the English a translation; the pages say that where
  they differ, the version more favourable to the user applies. Both carry the same clause ids; a test fails when one
  lacks a clause of the other; one `TERMS_VERSION`.
- **The console** speaks English, its words written in its code. **The developer docs** stay English.

### The introduction surface

**Page types** (Vietnamese path; the English one adds `/en`):

| Page | Path | Holds | Structured data |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | `/` | the catalog first; one answering line — "Game nhỏ cho cả nhà · Không quảng cáo · Không cần tài khoản · Chơi ngay"; *Continue* ([ADR 0046](0046-player-surfaces-two-zones-one-frame.md)); a date | `WebSite`, `Organization`, `ItemList` |
| A game's page | `/games/<id>/` | *Play* and *Play with a friend* at the top; a one- or two-sentence answer (what, for what age, how long a round lasts); **a facts table** (ages, players, round length, ads: none, data collected: none — and plays offline once that is true of every game *(agent)*); how to play; pictures with `alt`; related games; a date | `VideoGame`, `BreadcrumbList` — no rating that is not real |
| The promise | `/promise/` | no ads, no tracking, no purchases, no coins — never | `WebPage` |
| A collection | `/games/c/<slug>/` | from about **eight games**; written by a person: an introduction that answers, a table comparing its games | `CollectionPage`, `ItemList`, `BreadcrumbList` |
| A page for a search | `/guides/<slug>/` | only with **at least three fitting games and written text** ("game cho bé không quảng cáo") | `Article`, `ItemList` |
| Guides, FAQ, about, papers | as today | — | `FAQPage` kept on the FAQ for understanding |

**One source per game.** A game's public page is written in its own folder, `games/<id>/page/vi.md` and
`games/<id>/page/en.md` — its answer, how to play, who it suits, its own questions, and the date it was last revised in
front matter (`updated`) — outside `public/`, so the games origin never serves it. Facts are never written twice: names,
ages, orientation, seats and whether it plays together come from `game.json`; facts the manifest does not hold and that
do not depend on the language, such as how long a round lasts, live once in `games/<id>/page/facts.json` *(agent)*.
Every game under `games/` gets a page, as today. The public pages' build reads every game's folder and makes the pages
in both languages, their JSON-LD and **a preview picture per game and language**, drawn by `generate:og` and committed
beside the game's page (the build checks that each is there and up to date) and served under the game's page
(`/games/<id>/og.png`, `/en/games/<id>/og.png`). The build fails when a game lacks a page
in either language, a picture or a date, when a language pair is one-sided, or when a collection has fewer than three
games. `/new-game` writes the files, and a game's Definition of Done includes them.

**Search engines and AI answers.**

- Facts live in visible text, with dates and tables; names stay the same everywhere; `llms.txt` stays, cheap, though no
  major engine is known to read it.
- `robots.txt` keeps the content signals, **names** the crawlers that fetch for search and answers (Googlebot, Bingbot,
  `OAI-SearchBot`, `Claude-SearchBot`, `PerplexityBot`, Applebot, and Cốc Cốc's) in a group that repeats the rules every
  crawler gets — a crawler follows only the group that names it (RFC 9309) — and **disallows everything** to the crawlers
  and tokens that are only for training models (`GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `CCBot`; the tokens `Google-Extended`,
  `Applebot-Extended`). The CDN in front must not block the search crawlers.

**Growth.** At four games the home page lists them all; at about eight, three to six collections; at about thirty, a
static search over a JSON index made at build time (not indexed) and a paginated list of all games; at about a hundred,
one sitemap per language under a sitemap index, and IndexNow pinged on deploy.

**Measured** with Search Console (which counts AI Overviews' visits) and Bing Webmaster Tools by kind of page and
language, crawler counts from the server's logs, Lighthouse in CI, and a monthly look at whether ChatGPT, Perplexity and
Google's AI answers cite Tò He for 10–20 questions.

### Not done

- No third language for the hub or the public pages.
- No page made only to rank: no grid of keywords by category, no rating that is not real.
- No language in the app's paths.

## Consequences

- `@tohe/routes`: the namespaces `/en/…` and `/promise/`, each with the pages that use it; the sitemap's pages in both
  languages with their `hreflang` alternates and `x-default`; `robots.txt` with the named crawlers. Generated files
  regenerated.
- `apps/web`: Astro's i18n routing (Vietnamese without a prefix), the English pages, the switch and the suggestion, a
  content collection over `games/*/page/`, a preview picture per game, the facts table, the promise page, and the build
  check's new rules (the sitemap's language pairs among them). The words in `apps/web/src/content/games.ts` move to each
  game's folder; `apps/web/turbo.json` adds `games/*/page/**` to the build's inputs.
- The hub: the language setting and its order; its language sent when it makes a share link or opens a table.
- The API: a language per share link (an additive migration) and per room (in memory, with the room); previews in it.
- The Game Contract: names per language, in their own ADR and pull request.
- `apps/hub/src/legal/documents.ts`: an English text beside the Vietnamese, with clause ids and the parity test.
- The console: English words, an item of the product backlog (`docs/roadmap/product-backlog.md`).
- Docs: [Surfaces](../architecture/surfaces.md) and [Hosts and paths](../architecture/hosts-and-paths.md); the
  `/new-game` skill, [Definition of done](../conventions/definition-of-done.md) and
  [Create a game](../guides/create-a-game.md), which send a game's how-to to `games.ts` today; ADR 0042's status line
  notes the amendment.

## Alternatives considered

- **English as the default** (`/` English, Vietnamese under `/vi/`). Briefly chosen, then reversed by the owner: Tò He
  is made for Vietnamese families first.
- **The language in the app's paths too** (`/en/play`, `/en/s/…`). The app is not indexed, so a path gains nothing for
  search, doubles every route, and forces one language on everyone who opens a shared link.
- **Redirect by the browser's language.** Google advises against it; Googlebot sends no language and would never see
  the Vietnamese pages.
- **Keep the how-to of every game in one file of `apps/web`.** Works for four games; at thirty, a new game would ship
  without its page or with only one language, and nothing would notice.
- **Pages per search intent generated from keywords.** More pages, but "scaled content abuse" in Google's terms.
- **Lean on `llms.txt` for AI answers.** Google says it needs no such file; answers cite indexed pages with clear text.
