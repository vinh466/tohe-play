Fire number blocks up into the hanging rack. Equal blocks that touch merge. When the gauge is full the machine shakes the rows together. Big blocks at the top leave through the chute.

## How to play

1. Drag the loader to a column and let go to fire the block up into the rack.
2. Two touching blocks with the same number merge into one bigger block.
3. Every merge adds pressure. When the gauge is full the machine shakes: the rows slide toward the centre and every pair that touches merges at once, for double points.
4. A block of 256 or more on the ceiling is shipped through the top chute, scoring points and making room.
5. The rack sinks toward the safety line. When all five columns are blocked at the bottom, the machine jams and the game is over. Try to beat your own best!

## Questions

### What if I leave in the middle of a game?

Your device saves the game after every move. Next time you open the game, tap Continue to pick up where you left off.
