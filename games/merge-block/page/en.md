Merge Blocks is a game of dropping number blocks into columns: two blocks with the same number that touch merge into
one bigger block. Easy for the whole family — all it takes is a tap.

## How to play

1. Tap a column (or drag the block and let go) to drop the number block you hold into it.
2. Two blocks with the same number that touch merge into one bigger block.
3. The merged block falls and can merge again: merges in a row multiply your points.
4. The game ends when every column is full. Try to beat your own best!

## Who it suits

Anyone who can tap can play. Good for a spare few minutes, and for anyone who likes merging numbers and chasing a high
score.

## Questions

### What if I leave in the middle of a game?

The game in progress is saved on your device after every move. Next time you open the game, tap Continue to pick
up where you left off.
