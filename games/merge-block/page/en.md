Fire number blocks up into the hanging rack. Equal blocks that touch merge, and when the gauge is full the machine shakes the rows together. Big blocks at the top leave through the chute — with four ways to play and a Workshop that keeps what every run earns.

## How to play

1. Drag the loader to a column and let go to fire the block up into the rack.
2. Two touching blocks with the same number merge into one bigger block.
3. Every merge adds pressure. When the gauge is full the machine shakes: the rows slide toward the centre and every pair that touches merges at once, for double points.
4. A block of 256 or more on the ceiling is shipped through the top chute, scoring points and making room.
5. The rack sinks toward the safety line. When all five columns are blocked at the bottom, the machine jams and the run is over. Try to beat your own best!

## Who it suits

Cổ điển is the endless ranked game, and the only mode on the leaderboard. Ca làm gives three orders from the machine (ship a 16, two 32s, a 64); each finished order offers one of three parts (a bigger pressure tank, a lower chute, more pressure per merge or a stronger shake), and the shift ends at the jam or at Về xưởng. Ca hôm nay is one run on a seed from your local date, the same blocks for the whole family that day, with a streak and a share link; it is unranked. Thư giãn is the same machine with no pressure and no jam, for as long as you like, and nothing is recorded. The Xưởng keeps the scrap and XP every run earns, raises the machine's rank from Tập sự to Bậc thầy, and unlocks part blueprints: no timers, no energy, nothing to buy with money.

## Questions

### What if I leave in the middle of a game?

Your device saves Classic and Shift after every move, and today's shift as you play it. Next time you open the game, tap Continue to pick up where you left off.

### Why is only Classic ranked?

The leaderboard replays a run move by move from the seed the server dealt. Shift and Today's shift add orders or a local date, so they are kept local; Classic stays the fair ranked game.
