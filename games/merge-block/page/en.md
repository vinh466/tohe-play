Drop number blocks into the columns. Two touching blocks with the same number merge into a bigger one. If you can tap, you can play.

## How to play

1. Tap a column (or drag the block and let go) to drop the number block you hold into it.
2. Two touching blocks with the same number merge into one bigger block.
3. The merged block falls and can merge again. Merges in a row multiply your points.
4. When every column is full, the game is over. Try to beat your own best!

## Questions

### What if I leave in the middle of a game?

Your device saves the game after every move. Next time you open the game, tap Continue to pick up where you left off.
