Tap Merge is a number-gathering game: tap a block, and every block with the same number joined to it merges into one
bigger block. No clock, no rush — for the whole family.

## How to play

1. The board is always full of number blocks. Tap one.
2. Every block with the same number connected to it, diagonals included, merges into the block you tapped.
3. The more blocks you gather, the bigger the new block and the higher your score. New blocks slide in from the top.
4. There is no clock: one tap is one move. When no merge is left, the game ends.

## Who it suits

For people who like to take their time: no clock, no rush, every tap is a choice.

## Questions

### Does a game take long?

A game can last a long while, and there is no need to finish it in one go: it is saved after every move, and next
time you open the game you tap Continue.
