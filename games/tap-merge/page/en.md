Tap a number block, and every block with the same number joined to it merges into one bigger block. No clock, no rush.

## How to play

1. The board is always full of number blocks. Tap one.
2. Every block with the same number connected to it, diagonals included, merges into the block you tapped.
3. The more blocks you gather, the bigger the new block and the higher your score. New blocks slide in from the top.
4. There is no clock, and one tap is one move. When no merge is left, the game is over.

## Questions

### Does a game take long?

It can take a long while, and there is no need to finish in one go. Your device saves the game after every move, and next time you open it you tap Continue.
