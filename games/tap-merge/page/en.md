Tap a number block, and every block with the same number joined to it merges into one bigger block. No clock, no rush
— and now two short ways to play, with one notebook that follows you across all of them.

## How to play

1. The board is always full of number blocks. Tap one.
2. Every block with the same number connected to it, diagonals included, merges into the block you tapped.
3. The more blocks you gather, the bigger the new block and the higher your score. New blocks slide in from the top.
4. There is no clock, and one tap is one move. When no merge is left, the game is over.

## Questions

### What is new?

Three doors, one notebook. Endless is the same endless board as before, still the only ranked mode. Journey has 24
short levels, each with a goal and a par for 1–3 stars; there is no hard move cap. Daily is one board per day for the
whole family, 120 taps, 1–3 stars from the largest block; missing a day is fine. The Notebook keeps 20 blocks from 2
to 1M and 11 badges: every block you meet in any mode stays there, with no coins, purchases or expiry. Each mode keeps
its own run; open Modes from the menu to continue the run in progress or start a new one. Trying another mode never
loses your Endless game.

### Does a game take long?

It can take a long while, and there is no need to finish in one go. Your device saves the game after every move, and
next time you open it you tap Continue.

### Does the old game change?

No. Endless is still the same 5 × 7 board, 8 directions, whole-group merge, rounded up to a power of two, the rising
floor, Shuffle and the leaderboard. Journey and Daily are extra doors and never touch Endless's score or record.
