A company of guardians walks the cloud road. The card board is the game: six cards against six, auto-advancing through each stage, and every stop makes the company stronger. No ads, no purchases, no energy.

## How to play

1. Open the game and look at the six guardians in three rows: the front takes the hits, the middle is flexible, the back is safer.
2. The company fights on its own. Tap a hero card on its turn to cast a skill, or turn Auto-cast off to choose yourself.
3. A win pays gold, essence, shards and gear; spend them on stars, gear and new cards in Summon.
4. When you hit a wall, change formation, upgrade gear or try again. No energy, no lost turns.
5. Close the app and the company keeps walking; come back for up to 12 hours of farm.

## Who it suits

The first release has one region, Đồi Sương, six guardians, twelve stages and one boss. Four regions, twenty guardians and two events are the road ahead; this page will change when they land.

## Questions

### What if I leave in the middle of a fight?

The game saves on the device first, then syncs when there is a network. Next time, the company continues from the stage it was on.

### Is there anything to buy or an ad to watch?

No. Summon shards are earned in the game; there is no real-money store and no advertising.
