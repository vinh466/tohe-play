Play alone against bots, or open a table for the whole family, each on their own phone. If you know Tiến lên, jump in. If not, you will pick it up.

## How to play

1. Each player is dealt 13 cards, and everyone takes turns.
2. On your turn, play cards of the same kind and higher than those on the table (a single, a pair, three of a kind, a straight…) or pass.
3. Four of a kind and runs of consecutive pairs can chop a 2 (chặt heo).
4. The first to play all their cards wins the round. House rules (chop chains, instant wins…) are chosen when you open the table.

## Questions

### Is it played for money?

No. Points only count who wins at the table. Tò He has no money, coins or items to buy or sell.

### What if we are short of players?

Bots take the empty seats, so you can play on your own too.
