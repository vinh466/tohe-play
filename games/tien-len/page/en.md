Tiến Lên is the Vietnamese card game Tiến lên miền Nam: play alone against bots, or open a table so the whole family
can play, each on their own phone. For anyone who knows Tiến lên, or wants to learn it.

## How to play

1. Each player is dealt 13 cards, and everyone takes turns.
2. On your turn, play cards of the same kind and higher than those on the table (a single, a pair, three of a kind, a
   straight…) or pass.
3. Four of a kind and runs of consecutive pairs can chop a 2 (chặt heo).
4. The first to play all their cards wins the round. House rules (chop chains, instant wins…) are chosen when the
   table is opened.

## Who it suits

For anyone who knows Tiến lên miền Nam, or wants to learn it. Play alone against bots, or open a table for the whole
family, each on their own phone.

## Questions

### Is it played for money?

No. Points only keep count of who wins at the table; Tò He has no money, coins or items to buy or sell.

### What if we are short of players?

Bots take the empty seats, so you can play on your own too.
