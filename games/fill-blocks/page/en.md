An 8 × 8 board, three pieces a turn, no rotation. Fill a row or a column to clear it and score — play an endless run, a 18-level journey, or today's challenge. No timer, no ads, no pressure.

## How to play

1. Drag or tap a piece, then tap a cell on the board to place it.
2. A full row or column clears and scores.
3. Clear again and again to raise the combo; a clean board pays a big bonus.
4. The journey has 18 levels, each with three goals and up to three stars. Endless is the open board for beating your own best.

## Who it suits

A small child can play with a tap; an older player has the journey, the mastery ranks and the daily challenge to come back to.

## Questions

### What if I leave in the middle of a game?

The game is saved after every move. Next time you open it, tap Continue to pick up where you left off.

### Do I need an account?

No. Play on this device at once; make an account when you want your progress on another device.
