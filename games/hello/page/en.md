Tap the dot as fast as you can before time runs out. Anyone can play.

## How to play

1. Each round lasts 20 seconds.
2. Tap the orange dot as fast as you can. Each hit scores 1 point, and the dot jumps somewhere else.
3. The higher your score, the smaller the dot. A miss costs nothing.
4. When time is up, see your score and play again.
