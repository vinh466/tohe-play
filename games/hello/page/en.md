Tap Dot is a reflex game: tap the dot as fast as you can before time runs out. Any age can play, and it is also Tò
He's sample game.

## How to play

1. Each round lasts 20 seconds.
2. Tap the orange dot as fast as you can: each hit scores 1 point, and the dot jumps somewhere else.
3. The dot gets smaller as you score. A miss costs nothing.
4. When time is up, see your score and play again.

## Who it suits

All ages: one short round to test your reflexes. It is also the platform’s sample game.

## Questions

### What is a sample game?

It is Tò He's smallest game, a model for people who make new games. It is still a real game: you can play it, score
and set a best.
