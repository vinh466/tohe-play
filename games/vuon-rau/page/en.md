A small vegetable patch to dig, sow, water and harvest. Nothing to lose and no rush; crops grow in real time, even while the game is closed.

## How to play

1. Tap a plot: the farmer walks over and digs it.
2. Tap again to sow a seed, then tap to water it. A crop needs water again each time it grows.
3. Tap a ripe crop to pick it into your basket.
4. Tap Cô Ba’s stall to sell your vegetables and buy more seeds.

## Questions

### Do crops die if I forget to water them?

No. A thirsty crop just waits; water it whenever you like.

### Is my farm still there when I close the game?

Yes. Your device saves the farm after everything you do, and watered crops keep growing while the game is closed.
